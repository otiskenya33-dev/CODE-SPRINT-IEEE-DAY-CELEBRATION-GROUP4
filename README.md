AfyaCheck AI is an AI-powered health companion that helps patients understand their
symptoms and receive appropriate general health guidance. Patients describing mild symptoms receive self-care
advice and guidance on when to seek medical attention, while predefined safety checks flag serious warning signs 
and alert caregivers through a dedicated dashboard. Caregivers can monitor patient check-ins, review symptom 
summaries, and identify cases requiring follow-up. The goal is to improve access to basic health guidance, support 
caregivers, and help patients seek timely medical attention when needed.
